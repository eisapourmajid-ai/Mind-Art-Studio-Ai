# INTAKE LOG — Initial Data Collection

**Purpose:** Temporary, verbatim buffer for every PART provided by Majid before the declaration
**"End of initial data"**. Nothing here is authoritative. Nothing here is final.

**Rules**
1. Every PART is stored verbatim, without editing, correction or reordering.
2. Each entry receives a sequential ID, a date, and a provisional classification tag.
3. Provisional tags: `IDENTITY` · `CORE LOGIC` · `KNOWLEDGE` · `WORKFLOW` · `PROJECT SPECIFIC` · `TEMPORARY`
4. No PART is analyzed in depth until Majid declares **"End of initial data"** (unless partial analysis is explicitly requested).
5. After the three core files are produced and approved, entries are marked `MIGRATED` with their destination.
